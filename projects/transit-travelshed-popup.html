Wait model

Initial boardings assume you can time a frequent route, so wait is capped at min(headway / 2, max wait). Transfers can't be timed — the feeder vehicle's schedule decides — so transfer wait is the uncapped headway / 2. A boarding penalty is added at every boarding, initial or transfer.
