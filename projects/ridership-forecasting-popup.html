Corridor Demand Index (CDI)

A composite score (1–5) summarizing transit demand potential across all census geographies within the corridor buffer.

How it’s calculated: The CDI is a population-weighted average of the Transit Propensity Index (TPI) scores for each block group or tract in the study area. TPI scores combine nine factors—population density, employment density, zero-vehicle households, poverty, seniors, disability, people of color, youth, and limited English proficiency—each ranked by quintile within the corridor (1 = lowest, 5 = highest).

What it means: A CDI of 4–5 indicates strong latent demand for transit. Scores are relative to your study corridor only, not a national benchmark.
